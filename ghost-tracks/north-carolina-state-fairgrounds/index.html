North Carolina State Fairgrounds

Raleigh, North Carolina  ·  Three Cup races, 1955–1970

Three Grand National races were run on the half-mile dirt oval at the North Carolina State Fairgrounds in Raleigh, and the last of them closed a door that stayed shut for half a century. Junior Johnson won the first, in May 1955, in a race rain cut short of its distance. David Pearson took the North State 200 in June 1969, leading 182 of the 200 laps. Richard Petty won the Home State 200 on September 30, 1970, and that afternoon turned out to be the final time NASCAR's top division raced on dirt for fifty-one years, until the series returned to a clay-covered Bristol in 2021. The fairgrounds gave up racing shortly after. The grandstand is still standing, still owned by the state, and still put to use every October.

The state historical marker essay credits the 1969 win to James Hylton; the full finishing order shows Hylton third behind David Pearson and Richard Petty, and Pearson is used here.
