Somebody Else's 1988

The paint for Sunday is already out, schemes from the year my dad drove four races for a team that folded inside it. He scrolled through pictures of his own decade for sale and said about four words. My shirt is from that year too, and nobody ever put it on a car.

Ol' Dogs and Mountains

Somebody gave me a shirt two Christmases ago with a saying on the back, and it took until Sunday evening to notice the saying was not a saying. It was a list. Everything on it was standing in the yard in front of me, and not one item on the list is worth bragging about.

— Sarah

Low Water

The river came up twice this summer, hard enough both times to move things that had not moved in fifty years. Now the heat is back and it has dropped far enough to hand over a gravel bar and everything the high water set down on it. None of it lost. Just relocated, by a river that never once asked.

— Sarah
