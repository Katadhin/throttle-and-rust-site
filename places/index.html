Places We Love

Places we actually go.

Not a list anybody paid for. Just ours. — Caleb & Sarah
Lenoir, NC

Happy Valley Filling Station

Five minutes from the house on foot, which is the whole reason it works. Walk down, no plan, let the afternoon land where it lands. On a Sunday they run a Carbonara pizza with a fried egg thrown on top and, if you're feeling reckless, a bump of caviar. We ate the entire thing without saying a word. Sarah still orders the Bianca.

The move the Carbonara. Sundays only, and yes, put the egg on it.

North Carolina

Famous Toastery

When a morning has earned the right to take its time, we point the truck up the hill to the Famous Toastery in Blowing Rock. Big plates, coffee that keeps coming, a patio worth lingering on with the ridge behind it. There are others scattered across the state and we won't tell you which to pick, but the one up the hill is ours.

The move breakfast, and don't watch the clock.

Cornelius, NC

Eleven Lakes Brewing

Down on the west side of Lake Norman, worth the trip when we're that way. Picnic tables, cold pours, the kind of place you end up sitting longer than you meant to. The Meg hauled a six-pack back to the porch one evening and it has been in the rotation ever since, which is about the highest honor a beer gets around here.

The move a flight at the picnic tables. Ask about The Big One.
